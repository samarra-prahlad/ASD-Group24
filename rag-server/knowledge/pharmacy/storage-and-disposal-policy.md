Pharmacy Storage and Disposal Policy

Pharmacy stock is stored in a locked dispensing area at room temperature, away from direct sunlight and moisture. Medications are kept in their original packaging so the name, strength and expiry date stay visible.

Pharmacy staff check expiry dates during every stock count. Expired stock is removed from the shelves immediately, recorded, and set aside for disposal by an approved contractor.

Patients may return unwanted or expired medication to the pharmacy for safe disposal at no charge. Returned medication is never restocked or redispensed.

Medication returned for disposal is logged with the medication name, quantity and date. Pharmacy staff do not accept returned medication for a refund except where the refund policy allows it.
