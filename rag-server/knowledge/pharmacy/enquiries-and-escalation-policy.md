Patient Enquiries and Escalation Policy

The pharmacy portal and its AI assistant answer operational questions only, such as stock availability, prescription status, refund and refill rules and pharmacy procedures. They do not provide medical advice, diagnosis or dosing guidance.

Clinical questions must be referred to a pharmacist or the prescribing doctor. Clinical questions include how much of a medication to take, whether two medications can be taken together, what a symptom means and whether a medication is suitable for a particular person.

When the assistant cannot answer from verified pharmacy policy, it should say that it does not have that information and direct the patient to speak with a pharmacist.

Patients who experience a suspected reaction to a medication should contact a pharmacist or their doctor. In an emergency they should call emergency services. Staff who receive a report of a suspected reaction must notify the pharmacist on duty immediately and record the report in the pharmacy incident log.

Complaints and dispensing errors are escalated to the pharmacy admin, who reviews the dispensing record and responds to the patient.
