# Pharmacy Refund Policy

Prescription medications cannot be returned or refunded once dispensed,
in line with standard pharmacy safety regulations, unless the medication
was dispensed in error by pharmacy staff.

Over-the-counter medications may be refunded within 14 days of purchase
if unopened and accompanied by a receipt.

Refund requests for dispensing errors should be directed to the pharmacy
admin, who will verify the error against the dispensing record before
processing a refund or replacement.
