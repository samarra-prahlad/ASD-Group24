## Cardiology

Deals with conditions of the heart and blood vessels, including chest pain,
high blood pressure, irregular heartbeat, and heart disease monitoring.

## Dermatology

Deals with skin, hair, and nail conditions, including rashes, acne, moles,
and skin infections.

## Pediatrics

Deals with the health of infants, children, and adolescents, including
routine check-ups, vaccinations, and childhood illnesses.

## Psychiatry

Deals with mental health conditions, including anxiety, depression, and
mood disorders, through assessment, therapy referrals, and medication
management.

## General Practice

The first point of contact for most non-emergency health concerns. General
practitioners handle common illnesses, ongoing health management, and
referrals to specialists when needed.

## Geriatrics

Deals with the health needs of older adults, including managing multiple
chronic conditions, mobility, and age-related health concerns.

## Orthopedics

Deals with the musculoskeletal system — bones, joints, ligaments, and
muscles — including injuries, fractures, and joint pain.

## Neurology

Deals with conditions of the brain, spinal cord, and nervous system,
including headaches, seizures, and nerve-related issues.
