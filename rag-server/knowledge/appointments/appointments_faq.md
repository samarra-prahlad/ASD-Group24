# Appointments FAQ

## How long is an appointment?

Every appointment is a 30 minute slot.

## What do I need to book an appointment?

You need a valid patient ID, a specialty and a date. Pick an open time slot with a doctor and confirm your booking.

## How do I reschedule an appointment?

Open My Appointments, click Edit, choose a new date and time, and click Request change. An administrator must approve the request before the appointment moves. Only one change request can be pending per appointment.

## What happens after I request a change?

The appointment keeps its original time and shows Change requested until an administrator approves or rejects it. If someone else books the new time first, the change is rejected and you keep your original time.

## How do I cancel an appointment?

Open My Appointments and click Delete on the appointment. The time slot becomes available to other patients straight away.

## Do I need a referral to book a specialist?

No. The booking system does not check for referrals. You can book any specialty directly.

## Can I book an appointment through the chat assistant?

No. The assistant can look up appointments and availability, but booking, cancelling and changing appointments must be done on the Book Appointment and My Appointments pages.
