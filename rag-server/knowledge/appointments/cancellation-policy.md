# Cancellation / No-Show Policy

## Cancelling an appointment

A patient can cancel an existing appointment. Once cancelled, the appointment
is removed entirely from the system.

## What happens to the freed slot

Once an appointment is cancelled, its time slot becomes available again
immediately and can be booked by any other patient checking availability
for that doctor and date.
