# Cancellation Policy

## Cancelling an appointment

A patient can cancel an appointment from the My Appointments page by clicking Delete. The appointment is removed from the system.

## What happens to the freed slot

Once an appointment is cancelled, its time slot becomes available again straight away and other patients can book it.

## No-shows and late cancellations

The system does not track no-shows and has no minimum notice period for cancelling. There is no penalty for a late cancellation.
