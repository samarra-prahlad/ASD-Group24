# Lab Test Preparation Instructions

## Complete Blood Count (CBC)
No special preparation required. Patients may eat and drink normally before this test.

## Lipid Panel
Requires fasting for 9-12 hours before the blood draw. Only water is permitted during the fasting period. Patients should avoid alcohol for 24 hours prior.

## Thyroid Function Test
No fasting required. Patients on thyroid medication should take it as normal unless instructed otherwise by their doctor.

## Blood Glucose (fasting)
Requires fasting for at least 8 hours before the test. Only water is permitted during the fasting period.

## Liver Function Test
No fasting required in most cases, though some doctors may request a short fasting period. Patients should inform staff of any medications that may affect liver enzymes.

## Urinalysis
Patients should collect a clean-catch midstream urine sample following the instructions provided by staff to avoid contamination.

## Chest X-Ray
No special preparation required. Patients will be asked to remove jewelry or metal objects from the chest area before imaging.

## HbA1c
No fasting required. This test reflects average blood sugar over the past 2-3 months, so recent food intake does not significantly affect results.

## Electrolyte Panel
No fasting required. Patients should inform staff of any diuretics or blood pressure medications they are taking.

## Vitamin D
No fasting required. Testing is most accurate when done consistently at the same time of year, since sunlight exposure affects vitamin D levels.
