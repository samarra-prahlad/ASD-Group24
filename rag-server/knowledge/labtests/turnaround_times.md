# Lab Test Turnaround Times

## Complete Blood Count (CBC)
Results are typically available within 2-4 hours for routine orders, or within 30 minutes for urgent/STAT orders.

## Lipid Panel
Results are typically available within 24 hours.

## Thyroid Function Test
Results are typically available within 1-2 business days, as TSH testing may require batch processing.

## Blood Glucose (fasting)
Results are typically available within 1 hour for routine orders, or immediately for point-of-care testing.

## Liver Function Test
Results are typically available within 24 hours.

## Urinalysis
Basic urinalysis results are typically available within 1-2 hours. If a culture is also ordered, those results can take 24-48 hours.

## Chest X-Ray
Images are typically available for review within 1 hour, with a radiologist report following within 24 hours.

## HbA1c
Results are typically available within 24 hours.

## Electrolyte Panel
Results are typically available within 1-2 hours due to the urgent nature of electrolyte monitoring.

## Vitamin D
Results typically take 2-3 business days, as this test is often sent to an external laboratory.
