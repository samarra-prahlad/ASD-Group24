# Lab Test Reference Ranges

## Complete Blood Count (CBC) - White Blood Cell Count (WBC)
Normal range: 4.0–11.0 x10^9/L
A result above 11.0 is considered high and may indicate infection, inflammation, or a bone marrow disorder.
A result below 4.0 is considered low and may indicate immune suppression, viral infection, or bone marrow issues.

## Blood Glucose (fasting)
Normal range: 3.9–5.5 mmol/L
A result above 5.5 may indicate hyperglycemia or diabetes.
A result below 3.9 may indicate hypoglycemia.

## Lipid Panel - LDL Cholesterol
Normal range: below 3.0 mmol/L
A result above 3.0 is considered elevated and associated with higher cardiovascular risk.

## Lipid Panel - HDL Cholesterol
Normal range: above 1.0 mmol/L (men), above 1.3 mmol/L (women)
Lower than normal HDL is associated with higher cardiovascular risk.

## Thyroid Function Test - TSH
Normal range: 0.4–4.0 mIU/L
A result above 4.0 may indicate hypothyroidism.
A result below 0.4 may indicate hyperthyroidism.

## Liver Function Test - ALT
Normal range: 7–56 U/L
A result above 56 may indicate liver inflammation or damage.

## HbA1c
Normal range: below 5.7%
A result of 5.7–6.4% indicates prediabetes.
A result of 6.5% or above indicates diabetes.

## Electrolyte Panel - Sodium
Normal range: 135–145 mmol/L
Results outside this range may indicate dehydration, kidney issues, or hormonal imbalances.

## Vitamin D
Normal range: 50–125 nmol/L
A result below 50 indicates vitamin D deficiency.
