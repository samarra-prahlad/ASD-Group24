# What Each Lab Test Measures and Why It's Ordered

## Complete Blood Count (CBC)
Measures red blood cells, white blood cells, and platelets. Ordered to check for infection, anemia, and general blood health.

## Lipid Panel
Measures cholesterol and triglyceride levels. Ordered to assess cardiovascular disease risk.

## Thyroid Function Test
Measures thyroid-stimulating hormone (TSH) and related hormones. Ordered to check for thyroid gland problems such as hypothyroidism or hyperthyroidism.

## Blood Glucose
Measures the amount of sugar in the blood. Ordered to screen for or monitor diabetes.

## Liver Function Test
Measures enzymes and proteins related to liver health. Ordered to check for liver damage, inflammation, or disease.

## Urinalysis
Examines the content of urine. Ordered to check for urinary tract infections, kidney problems, or diabetes.

## Chest X-Ray
Produces an image of the chest to examine the lungs, heart, and chest wall. Ordered to investigate respiratory symptoms or chest injuries.

## HbA1c
Measures average blood sugar levels over the past 2-3 months. Ordered to diagnose and monitor diabetes.

## Electrolyte Panel
Measures levels of key minerals such as sodium, potassium, and chloride. Ordered to check kidney function, hydration, and overall electrolyte balance.

## Vitamin D
Measures vitamin D levels in the blood. Ordered to check for deficiency, which affects bone health and immune function.
